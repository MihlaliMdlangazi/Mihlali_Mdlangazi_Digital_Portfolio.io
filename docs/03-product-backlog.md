# 03. Product Backlog: Industry Placement (Speak2Inspire)

This document represents the master Product Backlog for digital content management, website optimization, and social media operations executed during the Industry Placement at **Speak2Inspire**. Items are prioritized using the **MoSCoW** framework.

---

## MoSCoW Prioritization Legend
* **M** — Must Have (Critical requirements for core digital presence and immediate employer requests)
* **S** — Should Have (Important features that enhance user experience and engagement)
* **C** — Could Have (Nice-to-have enhancements if time permits)
* **W** — Won't Have (Out of scope for the current sprint/release cycle)

## Backlog Refinement Notes
* **Priority Shifts:** High priority was given to core structural pages (Front Page, Offers, and FAQs) as requested by the employer to immediately improve visitor conversion.
* **Execution Flow:** Tasks move from the Product Backlog into active deployment upon direct stakeholder briefing and review.
