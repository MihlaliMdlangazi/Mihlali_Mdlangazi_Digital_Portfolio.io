# Agile Team Charter: Industry Placement Portfolio (Speak2Inspire)

## 1. Project Vision & Mission
To maintain, optimize, and elevate the digital presence of **Speak2Inspire** by executing rapid content updates, redesigning key landing pages (Front Page, Footer, Offers, FAQs) via WordPress and Elementor, producing visual media assets using Canva, and driving daily social media engagement to empower youth through confidence and leadership coaching.

## 2. Roles & Responsibilities
* **Mihlali Mdlangazi** — Digital Content & Web Intern / Social Media Manager / UI/UX Contributor
* **Employer / Stakeholder (Speak2Inspire Management)** — Requirements provider, feedback channel, and final approver of web and social media updates.

## 3. Communication & Feedback Channels
* **Task Requests & Briefings:** Direct alignment with the employer via email, WhatsApp, or scheduled check-ins.
* **Content Delivery:** Immediate, direct publishing via WordPress/Elementor and scheduled posts across social media platforms.
* **Review Cycle:** Iterative feedback loops where employer requests are executed, previewed, and adjusted in real-time.

## 4. Operational & Workflow Agreements
* **Content Accuracy & Branding:** Ensure all website changes, FAQs, and service offers align strictly with Speak2Inspire's brand identity, mission, and tone.
* **Rapid Deployment:** Execute urgent updates and daily social media management with agility to match live marketing campaigns.
* **Asset Creation:** Design professional visual graphics, promotional clips, and video content using Canva before deployment.

## 5. Definition of Done (DoD)
A task, website update, or content piece is considered "Done" when:
1. The requested change (e.g., Front Page, Footer, Offers, FAQs) is live and verified on the WordPress production site.
2. Responsive design and formatting checks are completed for both desktop and mobile viewports.
3. Marketing assets or videos requested via Canva are finalized and delivered or published.
4. Social media posts are successfully scheduled or published for daily audience engagement.
5. Employer sign-off or confirmation is received.
