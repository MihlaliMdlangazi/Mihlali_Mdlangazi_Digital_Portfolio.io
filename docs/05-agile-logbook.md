
# 05. Agile Logbook & Retrospective: Industry Placement (Speak2Inspire)

This logbook captures daily operational execution, Agile ceremonies (retrospectives), stakeholder collaboration notes, and continuous learning milestones throughout the Industry Placement at **Speak2Inspire**.

---

## 1. Daily Operations & Ceremony Log
* **Daily Stand-ups (Self-Alignment):** Reviewed pending employer requests each morning, prioritizing urgent WordPress updates, social media posts, and graphic asset production.
* **Stakeholder Reviews:** Conducted ongoing syncs with the employer to review newly deployed pages (Front Page, Footer, Programme Details, Testimonials, and FAQs) and integrated real-time feedback.
* **Deployment Cadence:** Continuous deployment model utilizing WordPress/Elementor for instant publishing, backed by Canva asset design.

---

## 2. Key Milestones & Deliverables Log
* **Milestone 1 — Core Web Infrastructure:** Refreshed the live Front Page hero sections and optimized site-wide footers for seamless navigation.
* **Milestone 2 — Transparency & Conversion:** Built the dedicated **Programme Details** page so participants can evaluate offerings clearly, and integrated direct **payment buttons** to streamline user sign-ups.
* **Milestone 3 — Social Proof:** Launched the **Testimonials** page ("Real People, Real Results") to highlight authentic impact and build trust with prospective clients.
* **Milestone 4 — Multimedia & Daily Ops:** Produced custom promotional video clips and graphics using Canva while maintaining consistent daily social media management.

---

## 3. Sprint Retrospective (What Went Well, What Was Challenging, Improvements)

### 🟢 What Went Well (Successes)
* **High Impact & Autonomy:** Successfully designed and launched critical user-facing pages (Programme Details and Testimonials) independently, directly improving platform transparency and user conversion.
* **Rapid Iteration:** Leveraging WordPress and Elementor allowed for immediate implementation and testing of employer feedback without deployment bottlenecks.
* **Creative Output:** Seamlessly combined web content management with visual content creation (Canva videos and social graphics).

### 🟠 Challenges Faced
* **Balancing Scope:** Managing simultaneous demands between daily social media operations and major page redesigns (like the Programme Details build) required careful time management.
* **Responsive Fine-Tuning:** Ensuring custom-built layout sections looked equally polished on mobile viewports as they did on desktop required iterative adjustments.

### 🚀 Key Takeaways & Continuous Improvement
* **User-Centric Focus:** Building pages like the FAQ and Programme Details reinforced the importance of anticipating user friction points and addressing them upfront.
* **Agile Adaptability:** Operating in a live production environment taught the value of quick turnaround times, clear communication, and maintaining brand consistency across all digital touchpoints.
