I am Mihlali Mdlangazi

I am a final-year student pursuing a National Diploma in Information and Communication Technology: Applications Development (NQF Level 6) at the Cape Peninsula University of Technology in Cape Town.

I am currently on my Industry Placement, applying Agile/Scrum, TDD, SOLID, OOP, and data analytical approaches on real software projects. I also completed the CAPACITI AI Skills Acceleration Programme, including Google AI Essentials.
